How it works

Three things make a Qode easy to find and hard to misread.

Three-state dots

Each dot is empty, a ring or filled, so a small circle holds more than a square code of the same size.

Reads live

Point your camera and the scanner follows the code frame by frame, locking in the moment the text is certain.

Stays on your device

Camera frames and uploaded photos are decoded in your browser. Nothing you scan is sent anywhere.
